Islamic Banking & Calculation Methodology

Calculations are projected estimates based on standard Islamic banking profit-sharing principles (Mudaraba). Installments are assumed to be deposited at the beginning of each monthly cycle and compounded monthly.

Tax & Duty Notice: Projected values are gross returns. In accordance with Bangladesh Bank and NBR regulations, Govt. withholding tax (10% with TIN, 15% without TIN) and applicable bank excise duties will be deducted upon maturity.
Profit Rates: Rates shown are editable example rates reflecting typical Islamic banking trends (approx. 7.5%–9.0%). Rates are not guaranteed and fluctuate based on investment pool performance.
